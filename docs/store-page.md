# The Big Board

### Your empire, on one board.

Every building, business, employee, production line, sale and stock movement, in a single phone app — sitting alongside the vanilla apps.

[SCREENSHOT 1: full panel, BUSINESSES tab visible]

## Why?

The Big Board brings the numbers you check every day into one place, so you spend less time clicking through menus. It answers the questions you actually ask yourself at 2 AM:

- *Which of my shops bleed customers at 3 AM — and should it even be open then?*
- *Do I have enough fabric for next week's production?*
- *Who did I hire but never assign — and who's about to quit?*
- *What sold today, in which store, and what's left on the shelves right now?*

One tap on your phone. Seven tabs. No more guessing.

[SCREENSHOT 2: phone FullMenu with The Big Board button highlighted]

## What's inside

🏢 **BUILDINGS** — a census of every known building: address, district, type, size, traffic, capacity, price and status. Filter by rent / sale / operating / yours, sort anything.

🏪 **BUSINESSES** — your whole portfolio with a health checklist per shop: open right now? every open hour staffed? marketing running? low stock? uniforms done? Plus one-click hiring, dead-hours analysis, and copying a whole weekly schedule from one shop to another.

👔 **EMPLOYEE** — one button assigns everyone you hired but never placed, to the right HR plan automatically. See who's unhappy before they quit, and order full crews through the headhunter without the paperwork pain.

🏭 **FACTORY** — production plan per product grouped under TODAY / YESTERDAY / LAST WEEK with PLAN / SELL / PROD and over-under percent, plus a supply view that tells you exactly how many days of each ingredient you have left, including incoming importer deliveries. Shows the selected factory's own stock and warehouse fill rate. Size your next order before you run dry.

🚚 **TRANSFER** — inter-warehouse logistics management: per-source routes with destination and product management, gradual adoption per source, and opt-in editing behind a toggle so nothing changes unless you say so.

💰 **SALES** — BEST SELLERS by default: per-product SOLD, PRODUCED, live STOCK, REVENUE, PROFIT and TOP STORE, with TODAY / YESTERDAY / LAST WEEK + REFRESH and an ALL + per-store filter. Every column sorts and resizes. Right-click any row for Inspect in STOCK. Prefer hunting a single product? Switch to SEARCH and see what each store sold.

📦 **STOCK** — live stock across all your warehouses, factories and stores, with IN/OUT flows per building. Even shows products with zero stock but an active recipe, contract or route — so you know *why* the shelf is empty.

## How to use

1. **Subscribe** here on the Workshop (the game downloads it automatically).
2. Launch the game and enable **The Big Board** under **Mods** in the main menu.
3. Open your phone's FullMenu and tap **The Big Board**.

That's it. No keybinds to memorize, no external tools, no config files.

## Safe with your saves

- Built on the **official mod API** — no loaders, no hacks, no replaced game files.
- Everything the mod changes goes through the game's own systems, so saves stay clean.
- Add it to an existing save mid-playthrough. Removing it is just unsubscribe + restart.
- Achievements keep working.
