## 3. Version 0.10.0 beta (DRAFTS — do NOT post without explicit approval)

### 3a. Workshop changelog (paste into the Workshop update notes)

```text
0.10.0 beta — SALES grows a BEST SELLERS view

- SALES now opens on BEST SELLERS: every product you sold in the period,
  one row each with SOLD, PRODUCED, live STOCK, REVENUE, PROFIT and TOP STORE.
- TODAY / YESTERDAY / LAST WEEK + REFRESH, ALL stores or one store at a time.
- Every column sorts and resizes.
- Right-click any row for "Inspect in STOCK" — jumps straight to that
  product's stock flows.
- PROFIT fix: factory-made goods that cost you nothing on paper now use a
  wholesale-price fallback, so PROFIT no longer just mirrors REVENUE.
  (Services still show cost 0, so profit = revenue — that's correct.)
- The old per-store product search is still there under the SEARCH sub-tab.
```
